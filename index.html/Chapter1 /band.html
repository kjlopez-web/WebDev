Take 6

My favorite musical group is Take 6, a Christian jazz vocal group known for their incredible harmonies and gospel-inspired music. Their music combines jazz, gospel, and R&B styles and showcases strong musical talent and creativity.

Members of the Group

  • Claude McKnight
  • Mark Kibble
  • Joel Kibble
  • Dave Thomas
  • Alvin Chea
  • Kristian Dentley

Visit their official website: Take 6 Official Website

Favorite Albums

Take 6 (1988)
This debut album introduced the world to Take 6's unique vocal harmony style. It blends jazz and gospel elements and remains one of their most influential albums.
So Much 2 Say (1990)
This album helped the group gain even more popularity and includes powerful harmonies and uplifting messages in their music.
Join the Band (1994)
A creative album that mixes vocal jazz with contemporary gospel sounds while maintaining the group's signature harmonies.
